# HUMAN2AI OS

### Power Industrial Park

**An operating system for the way your AI works — in one copy-and-paste prompt.**

HUMAN2AI OS isn't another chatbot, a separate app, or a collection of clever questions to ask an AI. It gives the AI you already use **a working agreement and an operating framework**: start with what you want to accomplish, then identify the right knowledge, expertise, methods, tools, and path to a usable result.

**The goal isn't the fastest answer. It's meeting your actual need.** A simple question deserves a simple answer. A complex or poorly defined problem may take longer to understand than to carry out. That effort is worthwhile when it prevents a wrong solution and a costly restart.

**You can start with a single sentence.** You don't have to write a perfect specification on day one. The goal is to reach a point where **you and the AI understand the same task and the same outcome**. From there, execution may need nothing more than a brief go-ahead.

**Grown-up to grown-up.** No corporate spin, no trendy talking points, no flattery. If the premise doesn't hold up, the AI should say so. If its own approach fails, it should own the miss and find a better one. **You keep the final say.**

## ⚡ First run: one minute, one real task

1. Open **[HUMAN2AI-OS.txt](HUMAN2AI-OS.txt)** and copy **the entire prompt**.
2. Paste it into a new conversation with the AI you already use, such as ChatGPT, Claude, or Gemini.
3. In the next message, tell it **what outcome you want**. Use normal language. You don't have to select the expert, skill, or tool.

**Want something to try right now?**

> I want a simple website for my service. My budget and time are limited, and I'm not a developer. Don't automatically recommend a new app or paid subscription. Find out what I actually need, what already works, and what I can realistically put to use with the lowest total cost and risk. If an assumption is wrong, tell me and look for a better route.

**What should you watch for?** Not how impressively the AI explains its process. Look for whether it clarifies the real objective, spots a faulty assumption, checks existing solutions, and makes progress toward a verifiable result.

**We recommend the English prompt as the default.** For many LLMs, English instructions can be a useful starting point and may use tokens more efficiently. The advantage depends on the model and the task. Your conversation and final output can still be entirely in your own language.

## 🧰 What does this AI operating system actually organize?

These aren't separately billed modules, and the AI shouldn't mechanically run all of them on every request. One AI selects the capabilities that matter to the task.

| Capability | What it means for you |
| --- | --- |
| **Intent Codec + OHYL — intent and root cause** | Goes beyond decoding your sentence. It looks at your motivation, circumstances, unstated expectations, and the **right number of “why?” questions** to uncover the real need — potentially one you couldn't yet describe with your current knowledge or imagination. Assumptions stay separate from confirmed facts. |
| **Precedence — what takes priority?** | Distinguishes your latest decision, the standing agreement, the authoritative source, and advice you already rejected. An old answer doesn't override your accepted objective. |
| **Precedent — what already works?** | Looks first for existing solutions, reusable components, standards, practical methods, or repeatable experience. Designs or builds only what remains missing. |
| **TCO — your least-cost *reliable* route** | Accounts for your skills, time, money, access, and tolerance for risk. A paid SaaS product or boxed solution might be unnecessary if a built-in feature, a prompt, or a little code will do the job. In other cases, paying for a service may be the smarter choice. The goal is a dependable, sustainable result at the lowest overall cost to **you**. |
| **AllGAP + ReDiscovery — finding connections you didn't know to look for** | Connects human, technical, business, scientific, legal, and other relevant perspectives. It may adapt an older solution from an entirely different field. The value is in the underlying mechanism, not the number of ideas generated. |
| **PowerHUB — capabilities assembled around your task** | **Within one AI**, it adjusts professional perspectives, functions, skills, methods, tool choices, and the way work is organized. PowerUser and PowerTeam draw on traceable real-world expertise instead of pretending that independent agents or fictional specialists are having a meeting. |
| **Supervisor — staying on target** | Keeps the agreed outcome in view. When the work drifts, repeats itself, relies on a false premise, or reaches a dead end, it returns to the last valid shared state. |
| **Actual execution** | When the AI has the tools and permission, it creates the requested text, analysis, plan, file, or action — instead of merely explaining how you could do it. |
| **Evidence and capability limits** | Separates facts from assumptions and unverified claims. Doesn't report work as completed when it wasn't done, invent access, or quietly ignore the lack of live web or external authorization. |
| **Model and reasoning-mode fit** | May recommend a more suitable specialized AI model or agent tool and prepare a **portable migration brief**. If you stay, it adapts the method to the model, access, and reasoning options you actually have. It cannot transform one model into another or silently unlock restricted capabilities. |
| **Learning from success and failure** | Turns feedback — including **“this isn't the right approach for this situation”** — into a reusable lesson when supported by accessible memory or a canonical file. |
| **Context and continuity** | Keeps accepted decisions and original sources in view while reducing the influence of dead ends. LIGHT/HARD context cleanup does not claim to physically erase hidden model tokens. |

**You don't need to know which expert, method, or AI tool to use. Finding, identifying, and selecting what fits is the operating system's job.**

## 🤝 Grown-up to grown-up. Straight answers. Real accountability.

**Your end of the deal:** the outcome you want, the limits, permissions, what counts as done, and the final call. **The AI's end:** figure out what you really mean, find the root cause, check existing solutions, choose the right capabilities, carry out what it can, and show what actually happened. Neither side needs a pep talk. Both need to mean the same thing by *the job*.

**No political-correctness theater. No PR varnish.** HUMAN2AI OS does not treat mainstream opinion, corporate euphemisms, fashionable labels, or a person's credentials as a substitute for facts. A weak premise gets challenged. A bad fit gets called a bad fit. A hard truth isn't softened until it loses its meaning. That doesn't call for insults, discrimination, reckless claims, or pretending the AI can ignore its service rules. **The boundaries are the law, basic decency, the user's authority, and the platform's actual safeguards.** Respect for grown-ups includes the right to disagree and the duty to show your work.

**“USA Midwest” is a standard of conduct, not a ZIP code or a costume.** Show up prepared. Keep your word. Respect other people's time and money. Judge claims by what holds up in the real world, not by the name on the box. Own mistakes. Do the work you said you'd do. Anyone, from anywhere, can work this way; no accent, nationality, faith, or background required. The English in this project follows that approach: natural American speech, a little edge where it helps, never made-up folksy slang.

A one-sentence request is enough to **start**, not a substitute for agreement. If understanding the real need takes more effort than doing the work, spend it when the stakes justify it. If the job is simple, **keep it simple**. The test is whether the user's need was met—not whether the AI sounded impressive.

The **Power Industrial Park** subtitle describes the work: you wouldn't walk into a capable shop expected to bring the staffing plan, the tool inventory, and a complete set of instructions. You tell the shop what needs to be done. The shop figures out the workable route. Here, that operating discipline lives **inside one active AI**.

## 🧠 Social Brain and planned personal memory

**Social Brain:** community knowledge built from shared, tested operating patterns. The important link isn't just files in a database. It's **task → interaction → consequence → lesson → changed behavior next time**. A well-documented failure is useful when it shows where an approach does *not* work. Public release requires evidence, permission, and privacy review.

**Personal pattern library (planned):** a user-controlled record of your goals, preferences, working patterns, and confirmed feedback. It is **not automatically shared with Social Brain**. Real operation requires supported memory or storage access. **This is a development direction, not a finished product feature.**

Short-lived **PROJECT** state, lasting **DEV** lessons, and publishable **SOCIAL** patterns serve different purposes. Their canonical source can be a file, cloud service, or memory system **actually supported by both you and the AI**. No pretend automatic synchronization.

## ℹ️ Before you use it

- **Nothing to install.** It's a working agreement for the AI service you already use.
- **Not a multi-agent system.** It is portable across different LLMs; each run uses **one active AI** to organize the work.
- **Use the English prompt.** `HUMAN2AI_hu.txt` is a Hungarian reference for reading and checking functional equivalence, **not the recommended starting point**. Token differences vary by model; no universal fivefold saving is claimed.
- **The prompt doesn't grant new access.** Web browsing, storage, paid services, reasoning modes, and other agents depend on your platform and permissions.
- **Better organization isn't a guarantee of correctness.** Verify important claims and completed actions. A prompt cannot bypass laws, basic ethical duties, or the AI service's real safety restrictions.
- **Still evolving.** Real use and user feedback will determine which changes are worth making.
